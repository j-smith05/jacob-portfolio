Page-Load Network Analyzer

Oct. 2026

C Sockets TCP/IP DNS Bash

A C command-line tool built on POSIX sockets that fetches pages over raw HTTP and times each stage of the load — DNS lookup, TCP connect, time to first byte, and total transfer. Used it to measure Debian mirrors across the U.S., Europe, and Japan from three vantage points (laptop, university server, and cloud Codespace), then compared results against speed-of-light propagation limits to analyze routing, caching, and NAT effects.

Academic project — not publicly available.

Reliable Data Transfer over UDP

Oct. 2026

C UDP Go-Back-N Checksums

A Go-Back-N sender and receiver in C that reliably transfers files over UDP through a relay that randomly drops, corrupts, and duplicates packets. Detects tampered data with the Internet checksum, recovers with sequence numbers, ACKs, and retransmission timeouts, and was benchmarked across window sizes and loss rates.
