About Me

Experience, education, and skills.

Originally from Spokane, Washington, I moved to Boise to study Computer Science at Boise State University with a Cybersecurity Emphasis. My coursework spans defensive security, forensics, information assurance, and computer networks, and I put it to work in hands-on projects, from analyzing 194,000+ SSH log entries for brute-force attacks to building network tools in C on raw sockets.

At St. Luke's Health System, my work centers on two areas. Outside of work and class, I practice on TryHackMe and Hack The Box and stay involved in the CyberAI Club. I'm especially interested in network security, ethical hacking, and cybersecurity policy.
